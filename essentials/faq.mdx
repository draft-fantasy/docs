### How much does Draft Fantasy cost?

Pricing varies by game. The World Cup is free to play. The Premier League requires an upgrade after the draft to unlock the full season. Full pricing for each game is available on the [website](https://draftfantasy.com).

### What's the difference between the Free and Premium version of the Premier League game?

You can only draft in the free version. It's a way to get a feel for the platform and see if it's a good fit for your league.
The paid version unlocks the entire platform, allowing you to change your team, score points, and make trades. You can upgrade your league to premium at any time.

### What happens if only part of my league upgrade to premium?

The league will remain in the free version until everyone upgrades to the paid version.
You can also purchase the single league package which automatically upgrades your league to the paid version.

### How is the winner decided when a cup tie finishes level?

It is not random. Fantasy points are added together across every leg of the tie. If both teams have the same total, the higher-seeded team advances.

For an automatically seeded cup, seeds are taken from the league standings at the end of the gameweek before the cup starts:

- In a head-to-head league, teams are ranked by head-to-head table points, then by total fantasy points.
- In a points league, teams are ranked by total fantasy points.

For a manually paired cup without stored seeds, a level tie is decided by total fantasy points in the league, then by head-to-head table points. If every value is still equal, the game uses a consistent internal fallback based on the team IDs. This final fallback is deterministic, not random.
