### How much does Draft Fantasy cost?

Pricing varies by competition. Some games are free to play, while paid games require Premium after the draft to unlock the full season. Check the `Pricing` page in the game you want to play for current prices.

### What's the difference between Free and Premium?

For paid competitions, the free version lets you complete the draft and see if the platform is a good fit for your league.
Premium unlocks full league gameplay for the competition, including team changes, scoring, and trades. You can upgrade your league at any time.

### What happens if only part of my league upgrades to Premium?

The league will remain in the free version until everyone upgrades to the paid version.
You can also purchase the single league package which automatically upgrades your league to the paid version.

### What's the difference between Premium and Pro?

Premium unlocks the season for a paid league. Pro is an optional individual subscription for advanced features such as saved transfer views and additional stats. Pro is not required to play.

### How is the winner decided when a cup tie finishes level?

Cup ties are broken deterministically, not randomly: aggregate score, then total fantasy points, then head-to-head table points, then a fixed internal team-ID order. Playoff semi-finals use the same order but fall back to a coin-flip in the rare case every value is level. See [Cups and Playoffs](/essentials/cups) for the full qualification, aggregate, and tiebreak rules, including the current behavior for tied finals.
