The player table on the `Transfers` page and in the draft room has a `Views` menu that controls which players you see and which stat columns are shown.

## Default Views

Everyone gets a set of built-in views: one per position, plus `Watchlist`, `Hide injured`, `Hide doubtful`, and `Easy fixtures`. The position views also adjust the columns — for example, the Goalkeepers view swaps attacking stats for saves and goals conceded.

## Building Your Own Views

Click `New view` to build a custom view:

- **Filters**: position, club, ownership, fixture difficulty, injured and doubtful players, your watchlist, and minimum thresholds on any stat — for example "Minutes is at least 500".
- **Columns**: choose from around 40 stat columns across scoring, attacking, defending, and general play. Columns your league's scoring doesn't use are hidden automatically.

You can duplicate any view — including the built-in ones — as a starting point, and edit or delete your own views from the same menu. Views are saved per team, so each of your leagues has its own set.

Anyone can try the view builder, but saving custom views requires a Pro subscription.
