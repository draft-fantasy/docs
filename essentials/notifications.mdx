## What We Send

- **Gameweek recaps** (email only): sent once the gameweek's points are in. One email covers all your leagues in a competition, with your result, league position and movement, your best player, and Team of the Week.
- **Draft reminders**: sent 5 minutes before your draft starts.
- **Trade offers**: when another manager offers you a trade.
- **Trade activity**: when trades are completed in your league.
- **New players**: when new players are added to the game. New players go on waivers first — see [Waivers](/essentials/waivers).

You'll also receive a few one-off emails when you sign up, create or join a league, and complete your draft.

## Managing Your Settings

On the website, go to `Account` → `Notification settings`. In the mobile app, go to `More` → `Notifications`. Your preferences are shared between the website and the app.

Each notification type has separate email and push toggles. For gameweek recaps you can also choose which of your leagues send them — handy if you're in a lot of leagues. Every recap email includes a link to mute that league directly.

## Push Notifications

To receive push notifications you first need to enable them on your device:

- **Website / web app**: click `Enable Push` on the notification settings page.
- **Mobile app**: tap `Enable push notifications` on the Notifications screen.

Tapping a notification takes you straight to the relevant page, such as the draft room or your trade offers.
